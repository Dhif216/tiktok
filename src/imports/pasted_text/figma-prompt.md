Absolutely — for **Figma Make**, I’d give it a prompt that defines the business, functionality, content hierarchy, and brand feeling, while explicitly telling Figma to make the **actual design decisions itself**.

Here’s a strong copy-paste prompt:

```text
Create a complete, modern, premium website for a TikTok creator agency that recruits, supports, and rewards TikTok creators.

IMPORTANT:
Do not follow a predefined visual style from this prompt. You are responsible for making the design decisions yourself. Choose the layout, typography, colors, spacing, visual language, animations, imagery, and component style based on what would create the most professional, trustworthy, modern, and attractive website for a creator-focused TikTok agency.

The website should feel like a real established digital creator agency, NOT a generic landing-page template.

BUSINESS:
The agency helps TikTok creators grow while providing support and protection for their accounts.

CORE BENEFITS FOR CREATORS:
1. Account Protection
   - Help protect creators when they experience account problems, restrictions, disputes, or other TikTok-related issues.
   - Provide assistance with disputes and account-related problems.
   - Make the creator feel that they have a professional team behind them.

2. Creator Support
   - Personal guidance and support.
   - Help creators understand and navigate account-related issues.
   - A direct support channel for agency members.

3. Monthly Coin Bonuses
   - Creators who reach predefined monthly coin milestones can receive additional bonuses/rewards.
   - Create an attractive visual section explaining the reward system.
   - Use example milestone cards such as:
     100K Coins → Bonus
     250K Coins → Bigger Bonus
     500K Coins → Premium Bonus
     1M+ Coins → Elite Reward
   - Make it clear that exact rewards/eligibility are determined by the agency.

4. Welcome Gift
   - Every new creator who joins receives a welcome gift.
   - Present this as an exciting onboarding benefit.

5. Growth & Community
   - Creators become part of a professional creator network.
   - Emphasize support, communication, growth, and recognition.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE:

Create a complete responsive website with these sections:

HERO SECTION
- Strong headline aimed directly at TikTok creators.
- Example messaging:
  "Your Content. Your Community. Our Support."
  or create a better headline yourself.
- Supporting text explaining that creators can join the agency and receive account support, creator assistance, rewards, and a welcome gift.
- Primary CTA: "Join Our Agency"
- Secondary CTA: "See Creator Benefits"
- Include a visually impressive creator-focused hero composition.
- Use TikTok-inspired visual cues without simply copying TikTok's website.

TRUST / SOCIAL PROOF SECTION
- Show statistics such as:
  "Creators Supported"
  "Coins Generated"
  "Monthly Rewards"
  "Creator Support"
- Use realistic placeholder numbers clearly presented as placeholders if necessary.
- Include creator testimonials with placeholder names/photos.

CREATOR BENEFITS SECTION
Create visually distinct cards for:
- Account Protection
- Dispute & Account Support
- Monthly Coin Bonuses
- Welcome Gift
- Creator Community
- Growth Support

Each card should have an appropriate icon or visual element.

HOW IT WORKS
Create a simple 4-step process:

01 — Apply
Submit your creator application.

02 — Get Approved
Our team reviews your application and contacts you.

03 — Welcome
Join the agency and receive your welcome gift.

04 — Create & Earn
Keep creating while accessing agency support and monthly reward opportunities.

MONTHLY REWARDS SECTION
Make this one of the strongest sections of the website.

Create an interactive-looking rewards/milestone system showing increasing coin milestones and corresponding bonus levels.

Example:
STARTER
100K Coins
Monthly milestone
Bonus available

RISING
250K Coins
Higher milestone
Bigger bonus

PRO
500K Coins
Major milestone
Premium bonus

ELITE
1M+ Coins
Elite milestone
Exclusive reward

Do not imply these exact amounts or rewards are guaranteed. Make the system easy for the agency owner to edit later.

ACCOUNT SUPPORT SECTION
Create a visually compelling section explaining:

"Your account. Our backup."

Explain that creators can contact the agency when they experience:
- Account restrictions
- Disputes
- Account-related issues
- Platform problems
- Questions about creator operations

Avoid making unrealistic promises such as guaranteeing that TikTok will restore an account.

WELCOME GIFT SECTION
Create an exciting promotional section around the welcome gift.

Headline:
"Join. Get Started. Get Welcomed."

Explain that approved new creators receive a welcome gift as part of joining the agency.

Include a CTA:
"Apply Now"

CREATOR TESTIMONIALS
Create 3–5 testimonial cards.

Example placeholder testimonials:
"I finally feel like I have someone behind me when account issues happen."
"The monthly rewards give me another reason to stay consistent."
"The onboarding was simple and the team actually responds."

Clearly structure these as placeholder testimonials that can later be replaced with real creator testimonials.

FAQ SECTION
Include questions such as:

- What does the agency do?
- Who can join?
- How does account support work?
- What happens if my account gets restricted?
- How do monthly coin bonuses work?
- What is the welcome gift?
- Is there a cost to join?
- How long does the application process take?
- Do I need a certain number of followers?
- How can I contact the agency?

APPLICATION / JOIN SECTION
Create a strong creator application form.

Fields:
- Full Name
- TikTok Username
- Email
- Phone / Contact
- Country
- TikTok Followers
- Average Monthly Coins
- Content Category
- Why do you want to join?
- TikTok Profile URL
- Submit Application

Include a clear privacy/consent checkbox.

Make the form feel premium and easy to complete.

FINAL CTA
End with a strong full-width CTA:

"Ready to Take Your Creator Journey Further?"

Supporting text:
"Join a creator agency built around support, rewards, and your growth."

Button:
"Become a Creator"

FOOTER
Include:
- Agency logo
- About
- Creator Benefits
- How It Works
- FAQ
- Contact
- Privacy Policy
- Terms
- Social media links
- Copyright

DESIGN / UX REQUIREMENTS:

Make the entire website responsive for:
- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

Use a strong visual hierarchy.

Create reusable components for:
- Navigation
- Buttons
- Benefit cards
- Statistics
- Reward tiers
- Testimonials
- FAQ accordion
- Form fields
- CTA sections
- Footer

Use subtle animations and micro-interactions where appropriate.

Include hover states, active states, transitions, and scroll interactions.

The navigation should become mobile-friendly with a proper mobile menu.

Make the CTA "Join Our Agency" consistently visible throughout the experience.

IMPORTANT BRAND DIRECTION:

The agency should feel:
- Professional
- Young
- Creator-focused
- Trustworthy
- Energetic
- Premium
- Digital-first
- Community-driven

Avoid making it look like:
- A cheap social-media template
- A cryptocurrency website
- A gambling website
- A corporate banking website
- An overly childish influencer website

The website should communicate:

"We take creators seriously."

Use high-quality visual imagery or generated placeholder imagery featuring diverse TikTok-style content creators, creators filming content, streaming, dancing, gaming, lifestyle content, etc.

Do not use copyrighted TikTok assets or pretend to be officially owned by TikTok. The agency is an independent creator agency.

Create the complete Figma design system and all major pages/sections needed for a production-ready website.

Also create desktop and mobile versions.

Prioritize excellent UX, conversion, trust, and visual storytelling.

Most importantly: use your own design judgment. I am intentionally leaving the visual design, color palette, typography, layout, imagery direction, and overall art direction to Figma Make.
```
